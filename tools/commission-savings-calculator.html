How the maths works

Saving = shifted revenue × (OTA rate − Stripe rate), where the Stripe cost is the percentage fee plus the fixed fee on every booking that moves:

  • Shifted revenue = annual revenue × share moving to direct booking
  • Bookings moved = shifted revenue ÷ average booking value
  • OTA cost = shifted revenue × OTA commission rate
  • Stripe cost = shifted revenue × Stripe percentage + bookings moved × fixed fee per payment
  • Annual saving = OTA cost − Stripe cost; 3-year saving = 3 × annual saving
  • Payback months = £1,999 ÷ (annual saving ÷ 12), rounded up to whole months

Worked example (matches our guide): on a £400 booking at a 15% OTA rate, the OTA takes £60.00; Stripe at 1.5% + 20p takes £6.20. The saving on that booking is £53.80 — roughly £54 per booking.

Assumptions — please read

  • OTA rate is an example. 15% is an assumed contract rate, not a verified UK-wide standard. Airbnb's host-only fee is 15.5% and Booking.com rates vary by contract; payment extras, partner programmes and discounts can push the effective cost higher. Use your own contract figures.
  • Stripe rate is for standard UK cards. 1.5% + 20p is Stripe's published standard UK card rate. International cards, other payment methods and custom pricing differ.
  • Only shifted bookings save money. Bookings that stay on an OTA keep paying platform fees. Nothing here assumes every booking moves.
  • Not the whole picture. The saving is the difference between OTA commission and card processing only. A direct booking website also has build, domain, hosting, software and marketing costs, and your own time. Do not describe the full booking value as money you keep.
  • Revenue and shift share are your estimates. The result is only as good as the numbers you enter.
